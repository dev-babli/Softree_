## 🧠 AI INSTRUCTION TEMPLATES — COMPLETE CATALOG

### **CASE STUDIES**

#### 📊 **1. CAR-R Framework — Complete Case Study**
**When to use:** Starting from bullet points or client interview notes
**Input:** Challenge/Solution/Results bullets, client industry, timeline
**Output:** Full narrative case study (800-1200 words)
**Quality gate:** Must include 3+ specific metrics, client quote, technical architecture mention

#### 📝 **2. PPT-to-Narrative — Transform PowerPoint**
**When to use:** Client provides PPT with key slides
**Input:** Slide content (Challenge, Solution, Results, Architecture)
**Output:** Polished case study maintaining slide flow
**Quality gate:** Each section expands by 3-5x from bullets

---

### **BLOG CONTENT**

#### 📰 **3. Pillar Content — Comprehensive Guide**
**When to use:** Creating cornerstone SEO content
**Input:** Title + 5-7 section outlines
**Output:** 2000-3000 word pillar page with internal linking strategy
**Quality gate:** H2 headers match search intent, includes downloadable template CTA

#### 🔥 **4. Thought Leadership — Hot Take**
**When to use:** Challenging industry conventional wisdom
**Input:** Contrarian thesis + 2-3 supporting data points
**Output:** 1200-1800 word provocative piece with practical implications
**Quality gate:** Acknowledges counter-arguments, includes "what this means for you" section

#### 🎙️ **5. Voice Search Optimization**
**When to use:** Content for smart speakers and voice assistants
**Input:** Existing blog post or case study
**Output:** FAQ-format with 30-50 word answers
**Quality gate:** Natural language questions, featured snippet ready

#### 🤖 **6. AI Overview Optimization**
**When to use:** Maximizing visibility in Google's AI-generated answers
**Input:** Any technical content
**Output:** Entity-rich, extractable content with schema markup suggestions
**Quality gate:** Every H2 answers a specific "What/How/Why" question

### **SEO & DISCOVERABILITY**

#### 🎯 **7. Complete SEO Metadata Package**
**When to use:** Publishing any content
**Input:** Title + body content
**Output:** Meta title, meta description, excerpt, focus keywords, header optimization
**Quality gate:** Title 50-60 chars, description 150-160 chars, keyword in first 100 words

#### 🏷️ **8. Smart Auto-Tagging**
**When to use:** Categorizing content for navigation and related content
**Input:** Full document content
**Output:** 5-10 relevant category/tag suggestions with confidence scores
**Quality gate:** Tags reflect actual content themes, not just keywords

#### 💬 **9. FAQ Schema — Answer Engine Optimization**
**When to use:** Any content that answers common questions
**Input:** Body content
**Output:** 3-5 FAQ pairs optimized for AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
**Quality gate:** Questions phrased naturally, answers under 100 words

## ✅ QUALITY ASSURANCE PROTOCOL

### **AI Output Review Checklist**

**Brand Voice Verification:**
- [ ] No forbidden words (cutting-edge, revolutionary, world-class)
- [ ] Active voice used 90%+
- [ ] Specific metrics present (not "significant improvement")
- [ ] Client/industry mentioned where relevant
- [ ] Technical depth appropriate for CTO audience

**SEO Optimization:**
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, 2+ H2s
- [ ] Meta title 50-60 characters
- [ ] Meta description 150-160 characters
- [ ] Internal links to related content (2-3)
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources (3-5)
- [ ] Alt text on all images
- [ ] FAQ schema included

**Readability & Engagement:**
- [ ] Flesch-Kincaid: 8th-10th grade level
- [ ] Paragraphs: 2-4 sentences max
- [ ] Bullet points for lists (3-7 items)
- [ ] Bold key terms for scannability
- [ ] Clear CTA at end
- [ ] "Before/After" or comparison table included

**Technical Accuracy:**
- [ ] Technology versions specified
- [ ] Security/compliance considerations mentioned
- [ ] Architecture decisions explained (not just listed)
- [ ] Human-in-the-loop for AI/ML content
- [ ] Bias addressed if relevant

### **Human-in-the-Loop Decision Points**

**Always Human Review:**
1. Client quotes (must be approved)
2. Revenue/cost figures (verify with client)
3. Technical architecture (validate with engineering)
4. Competitive comparisons (legal review)
5. Final publish approval

**AI Can Handle:**
1. First draft generation
2. SEO metadata
3. Image prompts
4. Multichannel adaptation
5. Translation (with spot-check)
6. Content refresh updates
