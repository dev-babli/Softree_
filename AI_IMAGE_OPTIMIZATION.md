- No cluttered or busy compositions

- No stock photo clichés (handshakes, meeting rooms)

subtle vignette, clean edges suitable for web use

### **3. Negative Prompt System**

**What the AI Avoids:**

**Universal Bans:**
- ❌ Text, watermarks, signatures, logos
- ❌ Human faces, hands, body parts
- ❌ Cluttered or busy compositions
- ❌ Cartoonish or childish styles
- ❌ Low quality, blurry, distorted
- ❌ Oversaturated colors, harsh shadows
- ❌ Generic stock photos
- ❌ Cheesy business imagery (handshakes, generic meetings)
- ❌ Outdated or retro styling

**Case Study Specific:**
- ❌ Playful, casual, informal, fun, comic

**Blog Specific:**
- ❌ Aggressive, jarring, shocking, clickbait

## 🎯 HOW TO USE

### **Auto-Generated Prompts (Recommended)**

1. Fill in your case study:
   - Title
   - Client name
   - Industry
   - Category (Power Platform, AI/ML, etc.)

2. Click ✨ (AI Assist) in document header

3. Select "🎨 Auto-Generate Image Prompt"

4. The system creates a **category-specific, professional prompt**

5. Copy and paste into "Hero Image AI Prompt" field

6. Click "Cover Image" → "Generate with AI"

### **Manual Template Selection**

1. Click image field
2. Select "Generate with AI" tab
3. Choose from 6 pre-built templates
4. Customize if needed
5. Select aspect ratio (16:9 for heroes)
6. Generate
