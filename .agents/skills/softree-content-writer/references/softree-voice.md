# Softree voice and tone

## Company identity

**Softree Technology** — B2B Microsoft and AI services partner. Offshore delivery speed + enterprise discipline. Not a platform vendor competing with Kore.ai, OpenAI, or Microsoft product marketing.

## Voice pillars

| Pillar | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Concrete** | Name the Microsoft surface (Copilot Studio, Entra ID, Fabric) | "Agents wired to SharePoint, Dataverse, and Fabric" |
| **Operator-trust** | IT, compliance, audit trails, governance | "DLP and approval gates before agents act" |
| **Delivery speed** | Weeks, offshore velocity, production not pilot | "4–16 weeks to production" (only if approved in brief) |
| **Honest scope** | We build and integrate; we don't invent platform features | "We implement agentic workflows your IT team can audit" |

## Tone

- Confident, not hype. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", "synergy".
- Short sentences in UI. One idea per card.
- Headlines: outcome or mechanism, not buzzwords.
- CTAs: action + role when possible — "Talk to an AI engineer", "Review our agentic AI work", "Start a Power Platform assessment".

## Typography habits (copy)

- Title case for product names: Copilot Studio, Power Platform, Microsoft Fabric.
- Sentence case for headlines unless design system specifies otherwise.
- Prefer comma or en-dash over repeated em-dashes.
- `{ Pillars }` / `{ Build }` brace labels: keep mono/editorial pattern if design uses it; swap inner words only.

## Headline swap test

Fail if replacing "Softree" with another company name still reads like generic B2B AI marketing.

**Fail:** "The AI-native foundation for agentic AI"  
**Pass:** "Copilot Studio patterns that ship in weeks with governance baked in"

Rewrite failures before emitting copy map.

## Approved recurring phrases (from live repo)

Use when they fit; do not over-repeat on one page.

- "enterprise certainty"
- "production-grade guardrails"
- "offshore delivery speed"
- "Microsoft stack your teams already trust"
- "not demos that stall in pilot"
- "human-in-the-loop governance"
- "auditable proof for IT and compliance"

## Forbidden patterns

- Purple/cyber "AI future" prose with no Microsoft anchor
- Competitor product names: Artemis, ABL™, ARCH, Kore.ai Agent Platform, "agentic era" as Kore uses it
- Fake product suites: "Softree AI Studio" (unless user explicitly approves a **services** page name)
- Invented client logos, "% faster" without source, Gartner/Forrester claims without citation
- Global string replace that alters `class`, `id`, `aria-*`, or URLs
