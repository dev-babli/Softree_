## Core Concepts

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) structures content, brand entity, and technical setup so generative AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews) cite and recommend your brand in synthesized answers. GEO success unit is citation/mention inside AI response, not blue-link ranking. Princeton GEO paper (KDD 2024) found right content tactics boost AI visibility up to 40%; strongest levers: citations (+30-40%), statistics, quotations, fluent authoritative voice. 44.2% of AI citations come from first 30% of page content — answer-first structure wins. GEO requires three gates: retrieved (technical/topical signals), ranked (authority/freshness/entity), selected for synthesis (extractable citable content). Perplexity rewards freshness (can cite new content within days); ChatGPT favors established authority; Google AI Overviews lean on search signals. Best practices: lead with 40-60 word direct answers; original statistics and named sources; clean H2/H3 question-formatted headings; FAQ/schema markup; quarterly content refresh; measure Share of Model not CTR alone.
