## Core Concepts

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures content so AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews) extract and cite it as direct responses. Distinct from SEO (ranked links) and GEO (presence in AI summaries) but complementary. Core tactics: (1) Answer blocks — 40-60 word self-contained summaries within first 200 words after each H2, written as complete quotable answers without transitional fluff. (2) Schema markup — Organization, FAQPage, HowTo, BlogPosting JSON-LD; pages with structured data see up to 40% higher citation rates. (3) Question-formatted H2 headings mirroring user prompts. (4) Entity authority — author profiles with sameAs links to LinkedIn/GitHub; consistent brand naming. (5) Evidence — statistics with named sources (+25-28% visibility per Princeton GEO benchmark). (6) Freshness — quarterly updates favored by AI platforms. (7) Technical — allow GPTBot/ClaudeBot in robots.txt, server-side rendered crawlable content. Measure Share of Answer across AI platforms not traditional CTR.
