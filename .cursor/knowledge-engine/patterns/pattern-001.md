## Summary

High-converting SaaS homepages place social proof (logos, metrics, testimonials) in the first viewport.

## Core Concepts

**Evidence:** Observed across Kore.ai, Stripe, Linear reference audits

**Confidence:** Medium (0.82)

**Observed impact:** benchmark +15–25% (industry range)

## Best Practices

Default: include 3–6 client logos or one quantified proof line above the fold on B2B service pages.

## Decision Framework

**When to use:** B2B SaaS and consulting homepages targeting enterprise buyers

**When not to use:** Brand campaigns where mystery/tease is intentional
