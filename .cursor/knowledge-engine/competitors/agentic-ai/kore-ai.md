## Summary

Kore.ai is Softree's primary benchmark for Agentic AI enterprise positioning, page craft, and component patterns. Not a copy target — a discipline reference.

## Core Concepts

### Positioning
Enterprise Agentic AI platform. Conversational AI, process automation, AI agents for regulated industries.

### Design language
- Restrained palette, rigid grid, enterprise credibility
- Strong typography hierarchy, generous whitespace
- Scroll-driven product storytelling on platform pages
- Pinned sections, progressive reveal, trust/proof sections

### Strengths
- Enterprise trust signals and industry vertical focus
- Coherent platform narrative across pages
- Motion purposeful, not decorative
- About Us and platform pages answer "why enterprise AI now"

### Weaknesses
- Not a consulting partner positioning (platform vendor)
- Softree differentiation: business-first consulting + implementation

### Ideas worth adapting
- Section narrative arc on platform pages
- Scroll/pinned interaction patterns (implemented in kore-ai-exact)
- Trust and outcomes sections before feature depth

### Mistakes to avoid
- Copying Kore branding/colors verbatim
- Platform-vendor language when Softree is transformation partner

## Best Practices

- Screenshot kore.ai before every Agentic AI page revamp
- Compare side-by-side in Verifier loop
- Reuse motion patterns from kore-ai-exact, not visual identity
