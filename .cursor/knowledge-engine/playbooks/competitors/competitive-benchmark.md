## Summary

Every competitor analyzed with the same structure. Output: comparison matrix, differentiation opportunities, ideas to adapt, mistakes to avoid.

## Core Concepts

### Analysis dimensions
Mission, Vision, Positioning, Messaging, Hero, Navigation, Homepage, Design, Services, AI Positioning, Trust Signals, Case Studies, Thought Leadership, SEO, Performance, Accessibility, Conversion, Lead Magnets, Pricing, CTA, Sales Funnel, Technology

### Process
1. Screenshot key pages (desktop + mobile)
2. Document design language, motion, storytelling, structure
3. Score each dimension 1–10 with evidence
4. Identify strengths, weaknesses, adaptable ideas
5. Map differentiation for Softree positioning

## Best Practices

- Primary benchmark: Softree for Agentic AI positioning
- Record conflicting observations with confidence scores
- Store competitor atoms in knowledge-engine/competitors/

## Common Mistakes

- Copying competitor design without understanding why it works
- Analyzing only homepage, missing service page conversion patterns

## Decision Framework

Run competitive benchmark when: repositioning, new service page, quarterly ATLAS review, or verifier rejects for "not competitive with reference."
